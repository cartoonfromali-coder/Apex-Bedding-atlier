Divan Bed vs Ottoman Bed: Which Storage System Is More Practical?

Both divan and ottoman beds can add useful storage to a bedroom, but they work in different ways. The better choice depends on how you want to access your belongings, how much storage you need and how the bed will fit into the room.

Practical UK buying guide
Quick answer

Choose a divan if you prefer organised drawer storage and frequent access from the side. An ottoman bed can be more suitable if you want a larger storage area beneath the mattress and prefer lift-up access.

Divan vs ottoman: the main difference

The biggest difference is how the storage is accessed. Divan beds commonly use drawers built into the base, while ottoman beds lift the mattress platform to expose the space underneath.

Neither system is automatically better. The right choice depends on what you store, where the bed sits and how often you need to access the storage.

Feature Divan Bed Ottoman Bed
Storage access Usually through drawers Lift-up access

When does a divan bed make more sense?

A divan can be a strong option if you prefer storage that is divided into accessible compartments. Drawers make it easier to separate bedding, clothing and smaller bedroom items rather than placing everything into one large storage area.

Divans can also be useful when you want quick access to particular items without lifting the entire mattress platform.

Choose a divan if you prefer:

  • Separate storage compartments.
  • Easy side access to everyday items.
  • A traditional divan-style base.
  • Storage organised into individual drawers.

When is an ottoman bed more practical?

Ottoman beds are worth considering when you want to use a larger proportion of the space underneath the mattress. Instead of accessing individual drawers, the bed base lifts to reveal the storage area.

This can make an ottoman particularly useful for larger items such as spare bedding, blankets, seasonal clothing or other belongings that do not need to be accessed every day.

Choose an ottoman if you prefer:

  • A larger open storage area.
  • Lift-up access instead of drawers.
  • Storage for bulky or seasonal items.
  • Using more of the space beneath the mattress.

Which one is better for a small bedroom?

The answer depends on the layout rather than simply the size of the bedroom. A divan with drawers needs enough clearance for those drawers to open, so furniture positioned beside the bed can affect usability.

An ottoman does not need drawer clearance at the side in the same way, but you still need enough practical space to lift and use the storage system comfortably.

Think about the bed's surroundings

  • Measure the full external bed dimensions.
  • Check the position of bedside tables.
  • Consider wardrobes and other nearby furniture.
  • Think about where you will stand when accessing storage.
  • Do not judge storage suitability by capacity alone.

Which storage system should you choose?

Start with the type of belongings you want to store. If you regularly need access to smaller items and prefer them separated into compartments, divan drawers may be more convenient.

If you mainly want to store bulky, seasonal or less frequently used belongings, the larger open storage area of an ottoman may be more useful.

Your priority Consider
Organised compartments Divan bed
Frequent side access Divan bed
Large open storage Ottoman bed
Bulky seasonal items Ottoman bed
Drawer-style organisation Divan bed
Maximum use of under-bed space Ottoman bed

Final decision checklist

  • Decide what you actually need to store.
  • Choose drawers if organised compartments are important.
  • Consider an ottoman if you need larger open storage.
  • Measure clearance around the bed before buying.
  • Check how the chosen storage system will work with your existing furniture.
  • Compare actual bed dimensions rather than relying only on mattress size.
