How to Choose an Ottoman Storage Box for Your Bedroom

An ottoman storage box can add useful bedroom storage without taking up the space of another large piece of furniture. Here is what to consider before choosing one.

Bedroom storage does not always need to mean adding another wardrobe, chest of drawers or shelving unit. An ottoman storage box can provide a dedicated place for spare bedding, cushions, blankets and other bedroom items while keeping the room looking organised.

Quick answer

The best storage box depends on how much you need to store, where you intend to place it and how often you need access to the contents. Start with the available floor space, then check the storage box dimensions, construction, finish and whether its appearance works with the rest of your bedroom.

1. Start With the Space You Have Available

Before choosing any bedroom storage box, measure the area where you expect to use it. This is especially important if the box will sit at the foot of a bed, beside a wardrobe or in another narrow part of the room.

Measure both the available width and depth rather than relying on the appearance of the furniture. You should also consider the walking space around it. A storage solution is only useful if you can comfortably move around the bedroom after it has been added.

Before ordering, check:
  • The width of the available floor area.
  • The depth available from front to back.
  • The height of the storage box.
  • The space needed to access the storage.
  • The walking route around nearby furniture.

2. Think About What You Actually Need to Store

Storage requirements vary from bedroom to bedroom. One person might mainly need somewhere for spare cushions, while another may want to keep seasonal bedding, throws or folded blankets together in one place.

Make a short list of the items you want to store before choosing your box. This makes it easier to decide whether you need a compact solution or a larger storage piece.

Items What to Consider
Spare bedding Look for enough internal space for folded duvets, sheets and pillowcases.
Blankets and throws A deeper storage area can make larger soft furnishings easier to organise.
Cushions Check the internal dimensions rather than judging capacity from the outside.
Seasonal items Consider whether you need regular access or only occasional access.

3. Check the Dimensions Carefully

Dimensions are one of the most important things to check when buying bedroom storage online. A product can look compact in photographs but still require more floor space than expected.

The EverlyBeds Ottoman Storage Box is currently listed at approximately 110 × 90 × 35 cm. Use those measurements as the starting point for checking whether the box will fit your intended position.

It is also worth leaving a little practical clearance around the furniture instead of measuring the available area down to the last centimetre.

4. Decide Where the Storage Box Will Be Used

The location can influence which type of storage box makes the most sense. A box positioned at the foot of a bed may become part of the overall bedroom arrangement, while one placed in a less visible corner can be chosen primarily for storage capacity.

At the foot of the bed

This can be a useful position when the bedroom has enough clearance. Because the storage box is visible, its fabric and colour can also become part of the room's overall look.

Beside existing furniture

If you are placing the box next to a wardrobe or chest of drawers, measure the combined footprint. Avoid creating a narrow passage that makes everyday movement awkward.

In a dedicated storage area

If appearance is less important in the chosen position, you can focus more heavily on capacity, dimensions and practicality.

5. Consider the Fabric and Colour

A storage box does not have to look like purely functional furniture. If it is going to sit somewhere visible, the finish can make a noticeable difference to the overall bedroom.

Think about the existing bed, headboard, curtains, flooring and other upholstered furniture. You do not necessarily need an exact colour match. A complementary tone can often create a more balanced look.

The Ottoman Storage Box available from EverlyBeds offers a range of fabric and colour choices, giving you more flexibility when matching it with an existing bedroom scheme.

6. Think About How Often You Will Use It

Not every storage box needs to be accessed every day. If you are storing seasonal bedding, you may only open it occasionally. If you plan to keep everyday blankets or cushions inside, convenient access becomes much more important.

Think about your actual routine rather than simply choosing the largest storage option available. A well-organised storage box that is easy to access can be more useful than one that is packed beyond its practical capacity.

A useful storage setup should:
  • Keep frequently used items easy to reach.
  • Allow larger soft furnishings to be stored without excessive compression.
  • Keep seasonal items together and out of sight.
  • Fit comfortably into the available bedroom space.
  • Work visually with the surrounding furniture.

7. Do Not Choose Storage Capacity Alone

It can be tempting to choose the largest storage box you can fit into a bedroom. However, bigger is not automatically better.

A storage box should leave enough space for comfortable movement around the room. It should also fit the proportions of the furniture around it. A very large box in a small bedroom can make the room feel crowded even if it provides excellent storage.

The ideal choice is therefore a balance between capacity, dimensions, accessibility and appearance.

Our Practical Checklist Before Buying

  • Measure the exact area where the box will sit.
  • Check the full external dimensions.
  • Decide what you intend to store inside it.
  • Consider how frequently you need access.
  • Leave enough clearance around the furniture.
  • Choose a fabric and colour that work with the bedroom.
  • Check the product specifications before ordering.

Final Thoughts

Choosing an ottoman storage box is mainly about finding the right balance between storage capacity and the space available in your bedroom. Start by measuring the intended location, work out what you actually need to store and then consider the box's dimensions, finish and everyday practicality.

If the storage box will remain visible, choosing a fabric and colour that complement the room can also help it feel like part of the bedroom rather than an afterthought.
