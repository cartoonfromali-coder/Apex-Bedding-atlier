Padded Bed Checklist

What to Check Before Ordering a Padded Bed

A padded bed can look perfect online and still be the wrong choice if its dimensions, headboard, storage or upholstery do not suit your bedroom. Use this checklist before placing an order.

Practical UK buying guide
Quick answer

Before ordering, check the complete bed dimensions, mattress size, headboard measurements, room clearance, upholstery, storage access, mattress compatibility and delivery requirements. Do not rely on the mattress size alone.

The padded bed buying checklist

1

Check the mattress size

Confirm that the bed is designed for the mattress size you intend to use. Double-check the exact UK size before ordering.

2

Check the full external dimensions

The frame can be wider or longer than the mattress because of upholstery, side rails and headboard construction.

3

Measure the headboard

Check both height and width. A substantial padded headboard can become a major feature of the room.

4

Check room clearance

Leave enough space for walking around the bed and opening nearby drawers, wardrobes and doors.

5

Review the upholstery

Look carefully at the fabric type, texture and colour and consider how it works with your existing bedroom furniture.

6

Check storage features

If storage is included, understand how it opens and whether you have enough clearance to use it comfortably.

7

Review mattress compatibility

Check any mattress depth or support requirements stated for the particular bed design before ordering.

8

Think about delivery access

Consider staircases, corridors, doorways and the room where the bed will ultimately be assembled.

Why external dimensions matter

One of the easiest mistakes is measuring only the mattress. The mattress tells you the sleeping surface size, but not necessarily how much space the complete padded frame will occupy.

Headboards and upholstered side rails can add noticeable width or depth. Always compare the full product dimensions with your room measurements.

Do not forget furniture clearance

A bed can physically fit inside a room and still make the room difficult to use. Think about the gap between the bed and wardrobes, bedside tables and doors.

Avoid this common mistake

Do not measure an empty section of floor and assume the bed will fit perfectly. Account for the mattress, frame, headboard, bedside furniture and the space you need to move around the finished bed.

How should you check the upholstery?

Upholstery is one of the main reasons people choose a padded bed, so it deserves more attention than simply selecting a colour from a screen.

Consider whether you want a smooth, textured, understated or more luxurious finish. Also think about how the colour will look beside your existing curtains, flooring, walls and other fabrics.

What should you check if the bed has storage?

Storage should be practical, not just an attractive feature on a product page. Check exactly where the storage opens and whether furniture will interfere with it.

If the bed uses drawers, allow sufficient side clearance. If it uses another opening mechanism, make sure there is enough practical space to operate it.

Check delivery access before ordering

A large upholstered bed may arrive in multiple packages or components. Before ordering, consider the route from the entrance to the bedroom.

  • Measure narrow doorways where necessary.
  • Consider staircases and tight turns.
  • Check lift access if you live in an apartment.
  • Make sure there is enough room to assemble the bed.

Final pre-order checklist

✓

Mattress size confirmed

The selected frame matches the mattress you plan to use.

✓

External dimensions measured

You have measured the complete frame rather than only the mattress.

✓

Headboard checked

The headboard size works with the room's walls, windows and furniture.

✓

Storage understood

You know how the storage works and have enough clearance to access it.

✓

Delivery route considered

The bed can realistically reach the room where it will be installed.
