How to Choose an Ottoman Bed for Your Storage Needs

The right Ottoman bed should give you useful storage without making the bedroom difficult to move around. Here is what to consider before buying.

Quick answer Start with the items you want to store, then choose a practical bed size, opening direction and layout. The best Ottoman bed is not necessarily the largest one; it is the one that provides useful storage while fitting your room comfortably.

An Ottoman bed can be a smart choice when bedroom storage is limited. Instead of using extra floor space for a storage cabinet, the area beneath the mattress becomes part of the room's storage solution.

However, there are several things to check before choosing one. Bed size, room dimensions, opening direction, mattress choice and the type of items you intend to store can all affect which model is most suitable.

1. Start with what you need to store

The easiest way to choose an Ottoman bed is to work backwards from your storage needs.

Think about the items currently taking up space in your wardrobes, cupboards or spare-room storage. If your main problem is bedding, an Ottoman base can give you a convenient place to keep bulky items that are not used every day.

2. Choose the right bed size

Bed size affects both your sleeping area and the potential storage footprint underneath.

If you have a smaller bedroom, a Single or Small Double may make more sense than using most of the room for a larger bed. For couples with sufficient space, a Double or King can provide a larger practical base while still leaving enough room to move around.

Always check the external dimensions rather than assuming that a nominal mattress size will be exactly the same as the overall bed width. The frame and upholstery can add additional width.

3. Think about the opening direction

Ottoman beds commonly use either side lift or end lift mechanisms. Your bedroom layout should determine which is more convenient.

  • Side lift: useful when there is good clearance along the side.
  • End lift: useful when the foot of the bed has better clearance.

Check wardrobes, bedside cabinets and other furniture before deciding. A storage mechanism is only useful if you can comfortably open it.

4. Check the overall bedroom dimensions

Measure the room before ordering the bed. You need to account for more than the mattress size.

  • Overall bed width
  • Overall bed length
  • Space beside the bed
  • Clearance at the foot
  • Wardrobe and drawer access
  • Space required to lift the storage base

A good bedroom layout should allow you to walk around the bed and access the storage without constantly moving other furniture.

5. Consider the mattress as part of the decision

An Ottoman bed and mattress work together, so check compatibility before buying. Different bed ranges may offer several mattress choices alongside the frame.

Current Ottoman configurations can include options such as memory foam, pocket sprung and orthopaedic-style mattresses. The important point is to check the exact options available for the bed and size you choose.

6. Decide whether you need a headboard

The headboard changes the overall appearance and proportions of the bed. Some Ottoman ranges allow you to choose between no headboard and different headboard heights.

If you already have a strong bedroom design, consider how the headboard will work with your wall, bedside tables and other furniture.

7. Think about how often you will access the storage

Not every item needs to be stored in the same place. Ottoman storage is especially useful for things that need to be kept accessible but are not required several times every day.

If you plan to store winter duvets, spare bedding or guest items, you may only need occasional access. If you intend to use the storage constantly, make sure the opening direction and surrounding clearance make this easy.

8. Check the construction and lift mechanism

Because the base is designed to lift, the construction and lifting mechanism are important parts of the buying decision.

Look for clear information about the frame, lift configuration and available options rather than focusing only on appearance.

What should you prioritise?

If you are comparing several Ottoman beds, use this order of priorities:

  1. Does the bed fit the room?
  2. Does the storage direction work with your layout?
  3. Is the size appropriate for your sleeping requirements?
  4. Does the available storage match what you want to keep underneath?
  5. Is the mattress configuration suitable?
  6. Does the design work with the rest of the bedroom?

Final thoughts

Choosing an Ottoman bed is mainly about balancing storage with practical bedroom space. Start by identifying what you need to store, then work through size, opening direction, clearance and mattress compatibility.

A well-chosen Ottoman bed can provide substantial hidden storage without requiring another piece of furniture, while still functioning as the main sleeping area of the room.
