Divan Bed Drawers Explained: Which Storage Setup Works Best?

Divan beds can turn the space beneath your mattress into useful bedroom storage. But the right drawer configuration depends on what you want to store, how much access you need, and how the bed fits into your room.

Practical UK buying guide
Quick answer

Two-drawer divans are useful when you want accessible everyday storage without taking over the full base. Four-drawer configurations can provide more organised storage, while drawer-free divans may suit bedrooms where storage is not a priority.

Why choose a divan bed with drawers?

A divan bed with drawers makes use of floor space that would otherwise be difficult to access. Instead of adding another chest of drawers or storage cabinet, you can keep selected bedroom items directly within the bed base.

This can be particularly useful in bedrooms where floor space is limited. The key is choosing a drawer arrangement that gives you enough capacity without making access awkward.

How many drawers do you actually need?

Configuration Best suited to Storage approach
No drawers Bedrooms with separate storage Keeps the base simple
Two drawers Everyday bedroom storage Good balance of access and capacity
Four drawers Higher storage requirements More organised compartments

Two-drawer divan beds

A two-drawer setup can be a practical middle ground. You get dedicated storage without using the entire base for drawers, making it easier to keep frequently used bedding, clothing or bedroom accessories organised.

Think about what you will actually put inside the drawers. If you mainly need somewhere for spare bedding and occasional-use items, two drawers may be enough.

Four-drawer divan beds

Four drawers make more of the divan base available for organised storage. This can be useful if the bedroom has limited cupboard space or if you prefer to keep bulky items close to the bed.

More drawers do not automatically mean a better bed, though. Consider whether all the drawers can open properly once the bed is positioned in the room.

Check the room before choosing a drawer configuration

Drawer storage needs clear floor space in front of the drawer openings. A configuration that looks ideal on paper may become inconvenient if a wardrobe, bedside table or wall sits directly in front of the drawer.

Before buying, check:

  • Where each drawer will open.
  • Whether furniture will obstruct the drawer fronts.
  • How much storage you realistically need.
  • Whether you prefer everyday access or larger storage capacity.
  • The overall external dimensions of the bed.

Are drawers better than a completely open storage base?

It depends on how you prefer to organise your belongings. Drawers provide defined compartments and quick access from the side of the bed. Other storage systems can provide a larger single storage area, which may be better for bulky items.

If your priority is organised access to everyday bedroom items, drawers can be easier to manage. If your priority is maximum open storage volume, it is worth comparing a drawer divan with an ottoman-style storage bed.

Final checklist

  • Choose the number of drawers according to your actual storage needs.
  • Measure the room around the bed, not just the mattress size.
  • Check that drawers have enough space to open fully.
  • Think about whether you need small organised compartments or larger storage.
  • Compare the available divan configurations before making a final choice.
