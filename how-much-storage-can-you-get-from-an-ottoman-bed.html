How Much Storage Can You Get From an Ottoman Bed?

An Ottoman bed uses the space beneath the mattress as hidden storage. But how much can you realistically keep underneath one, and what size bed gives you the most useful storage?

Quick answer The amount of storage available depends mainly on the size and construction of the bed. Larger Ottoman beds naturally provide a bigger storage area, while the lift mechanism determines how easily you can access it.

Storage is one of the biggest reasons people consider an Ottoman bed. Instead of adding another chest of drawers or storage cabinet, the space underneath the mattress can be used for items that would otherwise take up valuable bedroom space.

This can be particularly useful for spare bedding, seasonal duvets, pillows, cushions, blankets and other bedroom items that you do not need every day.

How does Ottoman bed storage work?

An Ottoman bed has a lifting base that gives access to the space underneath the mattress. Depending on the design, the bed can open from the side or from the foot.

This is different from drawers because the storage area can extend across much of the base rather than being limited to individual drawer sections. The result is a large hidden compartment that can make better use of the footprint already occupied by the bed.

Does a larger bed mean more storage?

Generally, yes. A larger sleeping surface gives the bed a wider footprint, which can translate into more usable storage underneath.

For example, a King or Super King Ottoman bed can provide substantially more under-bed space than a Single model. However, choosing the biggest bed available is not always the right solution. Your bedroom dimensions, walking space and mattress requirements still need to be considered.

What can you store in an Ottoman bed?

Ottoman storage works best for bulky but relatively lightweight bedroom items. Good examples include:

  • Spare duvets and blankets
  • Extra pillows and cushions
  • Seasonal bedding
  • Guest bedding
  • Bed linen
  • Soft furnishings
  • Items that are used occasionally

Keeping these items underneath the bed can free up cupboard and wardrobe space elsewhere in the bedroom.

Is Ottoman storage better than drawers?

Neither system is automatically better. They solve slightly different storage problems.

Drawers are convenient when you want quick access to smaller items. Ottoman storage is particularly useful when you want one larger hidden compartment for bulky items such as duvets and blankets.

Your bedroom layout should therefore influence the decision. An Ottoman bed can be especially useful when there is not enough room around the sides of the bed for large drawer openings.

How much clearance does an Ottoman bed need?

The lift direction is important. A side-opening design needs suitable clearance along the side of the bed, while an end-opening design needs enough room at the foot.

Before ordering, measure the available space around the bed and consider where you will stand when lifting the base. The storage may be generous, but it needs to be practical to access.

How to choose the right storage capacity

Start with the things you actually want to store. If your main goal is keeping spare bedding out of sight, even a smaller Ottoman bed may provide useful capacity.

If you want to store several bulky seasonal items, a larger size may be more practical. However, do not choose a larger sleeping size purely for storage if it makes the bedroom difficult to use.

  1. Measure the bedroom before choosing the bed size.
  2. List the items you want to store underneath.
  3. Check the bed's external dimensions.
  4. Consider whether side or end lifting suits your room.
  5. Check mattress compatibility and available configurations.
  6. Choose the largest practical size rather than simply the largest available.

Final thoughts

An Ottoman bed can turn the otherwise unused space beneath your mattress into valuable bedroom storage. The actual capacity depends on the size, construction and internal layout of the bed, so checking dimensions is more useful than relying on a generic storage claim.

If storage is your main priority, compare different Ottoman sizes and opening configurations before making your choice.
