Mattress Size Guide

How to Choose the Right Mattress Size for Your Bed

Choosing the right mattress size is more than picking Single, Double or King. You need to match the mattress to the bed frame while also considering bedroom space, sleeping needs and the people who will use it.

Practical UK buying guide
Quick answer

Start by checking the exact mattress size required by your bed frame. Then consider how much sleeping space you need and whether the room can comfortably accommodate the complete bed setup.

Start with your bed frame

The most important rule is simple: check the mattress size required by the bed. Do not assume that two products described as "Double" will always have identical specifications without checking their measurements.

If you are replacing an existing mattress, measure it carefully and compare those measurements with the manufacturer's stated mattress size.

How much sleeping space do you actually need?

A larger mattress can make a noticeable difference to sleeping space, particularly for couples. However, the largest available size is not automatically the best choice.

Think about how you sleep. If you move around during the night or prefer plenty of personal space, moving up a size may be worthwhile if your room allows it.

Measure the bedroom before upgrading

Mattress dimensions are only part of the calculation. The complete bed frame may be wider or longer than the mattress because of its construction, upholstery or headboard.

Measure these areas

  • The available width for the complete bed.
  • The available length from wall to furniture.
  • Walking space beside the bed.
  • Clearance for wardrobes and drawers.
  • Door and entrance access.
  • Space required for bedside tables.

What if you are replacing an old mattress?

Do not rely solely on the label attached to your old mattress. Measure it and compare the dimensions with the new mattress specifications.

This is particularly useful if your existing bed is older or if you are changing the bed frame at the same time.

Consider the people using the mattress

A mattress for a child or guest room may have very different space requirements from one used by two adults every night.

Couples should consider both sleeping positions and how much personal space each person prefers before deciding whether to move up to a larger size.

Do not forget the bed base

Your mattress and bed base should work together. Before ordering, check that the mattress is suitable for your particular base and that the dimensions match correctly.

This is especially important when buying a mattress and bed frame separately.

Final mattress size checklist

  • Confirm the exact mattress size required by your bed.
  • Check the manufacturer's stated dimensions.
  • Measure your existing mattress if replacing it.
  • Measure your bedroom before moving to a larger size.
  • Allow enough space around the finished bed.
  • Consider sleeping space for every person using the mattress.
  • Check compatibility with your chosen bed base.
